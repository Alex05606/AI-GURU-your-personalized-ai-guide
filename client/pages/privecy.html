Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

Welcome to AI Guru. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use AI Guru, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password (stored securely as a hashed value).
  • Chat Data: The messages you send and receive through the AI Guru chatbot, stored to provide chat history functionality.
  • Uploaded Files: Documents or PDF files you upload for analysis. These are processed in memory and not permanently stored.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical data such as your browser type and device, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the AI Guru platform.
  • Authenticate your identity and protect your account.
  • Display your personalized chat history.
  • Respond to your queries and provide AI-generated answers.
  • Communicate important updates about the service.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account data and chat history are stored securely in a cloud database (Turso). Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text. We use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) to protect your session.

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no system is 100% immune to breaches. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data only in the following limited cases:

  • With AI processing services (Groq API) solely to generate responses to your queries. Your messages are sent to Groq's API but are not stored by us beyond your chat history.
  • If required by law or a valid legal process.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Update your account information at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email below.

6. Cookies

AI Guru uses localStorage in your browser to store your authentication token and session data. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

AI Guru is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
